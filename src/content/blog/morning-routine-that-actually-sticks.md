Most morning routines fail because they're someone else's routine. The fix isn't more discipline — it's fewer, smaller anchors that survive a bad night's sleep.

## The three-anchor method

Pick **three** non-negotiables that take under five minutes each:

1. **Light** — get daylight within 30 minutes of waking. It sets your circadian clock better than caffeine.
2. **Water before coffee** — you wake up mildly dehydrated; a glass of water blunts the mid-morning slump.
3. **One line in a notebook** — a single sentence of intention. Not journaling. One line.

That's it. Everything else is optional.

## Why small wins

Habits stick when the cost of doing them is near zero. A two-minute version you do every day beats a 45-minute routine you abandon by Wednesday.

> The goal isn't a perfect morning. It's a repeatable one.

## Scaling up later

Once the three anchors feel automatic (usually 2–3 weeks), you can stack one new thing on top — a short walk, stretching, reading. Add slowly. Protect the core three.
