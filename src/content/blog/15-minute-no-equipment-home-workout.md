No gym, no gear, no excuses. This 15-minute routine hits your whole body using nothing but your own weight. Do it three or four times a week.

## The workout

Do each move for **40 seconds**, rest **20 seconds**, then move to the next. After all five, rest one minute and repeat. **Three rounds = ~15 minutes.**

1. **Bodyweight squats** — feet shoulder-width, sit back like into a chair, chest up.
2. **Incline push-ups** — hands on a sturdy table or wall to start; drop to the floor as you get stronger.
3. **Reverse lunges** — step back, lower the back knee, alternate legs.
4. **Glute bridges** — lie on your back, drive hips up, squeeze at the top.
5. **Plank** — forearms down, body in a straight line, brace your core.

## Warm up first (2 minutes)

Don't skip this. March on the spot, roll your shoulders, do a few slow squats and arm circles to get blood flowing.

> Form over speed. A clean, controlled rep beats ten sloppy ones — and protects you from injury.

## Make it easier or harder

- **Easier:** shorten work intervals to 30 seconds, do two rounds.
- **Harder:** add a fourth round, slow down each rep, or pause at the bottom of squats.

## Why bodyweight works

Compound bodyweight moves recruit multiple muscle groups at once, build real strength, and need no equipment — which means there's nothing standing between you and starting today.

---

*Listen to your body. If something hurts (not just "is hard"), stop. Consult a professional if you're new to exercise or have any health conditions.*
