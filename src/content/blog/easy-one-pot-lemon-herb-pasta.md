This is the recipe I make when I'm tired, hungry, and unwilling to wash three pans. Everything cooks together — the starch from the pasta makes its own silky sauce.

## Ingredients (serves 2)

- 200g spaghetti or linguine
- 500ml vegetable stock
- 1 lemon (zest + juice)
- 2 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- A handful of fresh parsley and basil
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 30g parmesan (or nutritional yeast)
- Salt and pepper

## Method

1. Add the pasta, stock, garlic, half the lemon zest and olive oil to a wide pan.
2. Bring to a boil, then simmer, tossing often, for **9–11 minutes** until the liquid reduces to a glossy sauce.
3. Off the heat, stir through lemon juice, herbs and parmesan.
4. Loosen with a splash of hot water if needed. Season and serve.

> Make it richer: stir in a spoon of mascarpone at the end.

## Why one-pot works

As the pasta cooks in just enough liquid, the released starch thickens everything into a sauce — no separate roux, no draining, no extra pan.
