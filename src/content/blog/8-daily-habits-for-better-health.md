Good health rarely comes from one dramatic change. It comes from a handful of small habits repeated daily. Here are eight that give you the most return for the least effort.

## 1. Front-load your water

Aim for a large glass of water first thing. Mild overnight dehydration is a common (and fixable) cause of morning fog and headaches.

## 2. Get morning daylight

Ten minutes of natural light early in the day helps regulate your sleep-wake cycle — which improves both energy now and sleep tonight.

## 3. Eat protein at breakfast

Protein keeps you fuller for longer and steadies blood sugar, reducing mid-morning cravings. Eggs, yoghurt, or beans all work.

## 4. Move every hour

You don't need a gym. Standing up and walking for two minutes each hour offsets a lot of the harm from prolonged sitting.

> Consistency beats intensity. A daily 20-minute walk outperforms an occasional punishing workout.

## 5. Eat the rainbow

Different coloured fruits and vegetables deliver different nutrients. Variety, not perfection, is the goal.

## 6. Protect your sleep window

Pick a consistent bedtime and dim screens an hour before. Sleep is when your body repairs — it's not optional.

## 7. Take a breath break

Two minutes of slow breathing lowers stress hormones. Try four counts in, six counts out.

## 8. Stand more, scroll less

Swap ten minutes of evening scrolling for a short stretch or walk. Small trades compound.
